BorderMe privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Short version: BorderMe doesn't collect anything about you. Your photos stay on your phone, and I never see them.

No data collected

BorderMe collects no data. There are no accounts to make, no analytics and no ads, and BorderMe never connects to the internet. There's no networking code in it at all, so there's nothing in there that could send your photos anywhere.

Your photos

Photos are processed only on your device. The borders are drawn right there on the phone, and the result is saved straight back to your own photo library. Your original photos are never changed; BorderMe adds new copies beside them.

Photo library permission

BorderMe asks only to add photos to your library. It never asks to read the whole thing. You choose which photos to hand over each time, using Apple's own picker, or by sharing them from the Photos app.

Location

Location data is removed from the bordered copies, because GPS coordinates are rarely something you meant to publish along with a picture. Copyright, camera, lens and capture date are kept. Your original still has its location if you need it.

Questions
